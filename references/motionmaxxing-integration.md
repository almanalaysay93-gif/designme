HyperFrames remains the default final film assembler. Remotion and Open 3D may provide assigned intermediate assets; they are not alternative final renders unless the deliverable explicitly selects them.

Do not require optional-agent approval for contribution/review tasks. Do not apply film-only no-page-chrome or continuous-motion rules to normal website UI. For 3D parallax sites, review layer/camera/scroll motion, text readability and mobile/reduced-motion behavior. Use actual evidence, assign repair ownership and preserve the three-loop cap. Free/local-only overrides and upstream licenses remain binding.
